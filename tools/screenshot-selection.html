Understanding Self-Attention in Transformer Models

The Transformer architecture replaces recurrent layers with attention mechanisms. Self-attention lets each token compare itself with every other token in the sequence, making it easier to capture long-range dependencies. This design improves parallel training efficiency and makes modern language models easier to scale.
